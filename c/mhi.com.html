How to use this

If you're applying: The MHI Talent Solutions roles are placements at client companies. Your employer would be the client, either directly or after a contract period. If you want to work for Myers-Holum itself, look at the remote NSPB, NSAW and NetSuite consulting roles. Note that the NetSuite consulting and pre-sales postings date from 2020-2021.

If you're selling or recruiting: This is Myers-Holum, a NetSuite consulting firm with a recruiting arm, not Mitsubishi Heavy Industries. Recruiters will be competing with MHI Talent Solutions on its client searches. Sales teams selling to NetSuite partners should note the tools named in its postings (Celigo, Boomi, Stripe, Zone Billing, Google Cloud Platform) and its NSPB and NSAW practices.
