What they're building

Institutional trading exchange build-out (Everything Exchange: matching, market data, risk, unified trading) strong signal

Today: Coinbase Derivatives Exchange (CDE) and Deribit (Market Operations postings) and Coinbase Financial Markets, its CFTC-regulated FCM (FCM posting)

Roles: Senior Staff Software Engineer, Institutional Exchange · Senior Market Data Engineer · Staff Data Engineer, Market Data · Sr. Staff Technical Architect, Unified Trading · Sr. Staff Technical Risk Architect, Unified Trading · Staff Infrastructure Engineer, Trading · Senior Infrastructure Engineer, Trading · Staff Software Engineer (Institutional, FCM) · Senior Network Engineer

Requirements mention: low-latency distributed systems · matching engine · market data protocols · Aeron · SBE · FIX · ITCH/OUCH · unified trading systems · multi-venue connectivity

Inference: the postings describe one unified trading platform running across cloud and on-prem/colocated venues, with shared matching, market data and real-time risk/margin engines across Retail, Prime and wholesale brokerage.

Mobile app rewrite from React Native to native Swift and Kotlin strong signal

Today: Coinbase's retail mobile app, which the postings describe as a React Native codebase

Roles: Staff Software Engineer, Mobile QA · Senior Software Engineer, Mobile QA · Staff Software Engineer, Native Mobile · Senior Staff Software Engineer, Native Mobile · Senior Software Engineer, Native Mobile

Inference: the mobile team is replacing the React Native app with native builds, with QA and performance work alongside it.

Regulated international expansion in Abu Dhabi/UAE and Singapore strong signal

Today: Tokenized Equities for eligible non-US users (Accounting Manager, Tokenized Equities posting) and a regulated business under MAS in Singapore (Operations Manager posting)

Roles: Senior Executive Officer, ADGM · Money Laundering Reporting Officer, ADGM · Operations Manager, ADGM · Accounting Manager, Tokenized Equities · Operations Manager · Regulatory Compliance Manager

Requirements mention: ADGM/FSRA regulatory requirements · building a compliance program from the ground up · tokenization · Familiarity with the FSRA · MAS Securities and Future Act

Inference: senior officer, MLRO and operations hires point to staffing a new ADGM legal entity and licensing work in Singapore.

Stablecoin and payments rails moderate signal

Today: Stablecoin payment rail for merchant checkout, subscriptions and onchain settlement (Software Engineer, CDP - Payment Acceptance posting)

Roles: Software Engineer, CDP - Stablecoin · Senior Software Engineer, Stablecoins · Senior Software Engineer, Backend - Payments Platform · Software Engineer- Money Movement · Group Product Manager, Money Movement

Requirements mention: stablecoin issuance · smart contracts · payment rails · fiat on- and off-ramps · self-serve APIs

Inference: the Payments Platform posting describes a shared payments API for Coinbase's Retail, Institutional and Developer teams, alongside partner-branded stablecoin issuance.

AI in customer support and compliance operations moderate signal

Today: Customer support through Help Center, chat and voice (Group Product Manager, Customer Engagement & Experience posting)

Roles: Staff Machine Learning Engineer · Group Product Manager, Customer Engagement & Experience · Senior Engineering Manager, Core AI Automation (Platform) · Forward Deployed Engineer, Compliance [Office of the CTO]

Requirements mention: LLM-based assistants · agentic workflows · intelligent routing · guardrails · multi-agent orchestration · transaction monitoring

Inference: agentic AI is being applied to customer support and compliance workflows, based on the posted requirements.

Agentic trading in the Coinbase app (Coinbase Advisor) and supporting account systems moderate signal
