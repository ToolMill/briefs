How to use this

If you're applying: Check your licence first. Every driver posting asks for rijbewijs D / permis D, and school and factory postings also ask for vakbekwaamheid / CAP. Posted pay is €16,59 an hour for school and BASF routes, €18,16 for TEC lines and €19,52–€19,92 for De Lijn lines. School routes are part-time.

If you're selling or recruiting: Most of the 28 open roles are bus drivers: 12 school routes nationwide (6 in Antwerpen province), 8 line-bus roles for De Lijn and TEC, and 2 BASF factory routes. Non-driver openings are few: two mechanics, a Depot Manager in Zulte and an HR Business Partner in Diegem. Confirm the region and language before outreach, and note that most postings do not state seniority.
