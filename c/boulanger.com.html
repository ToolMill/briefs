How to use this

If you're applying: Search by town and job title rather than by department, because the same title (for example EXPERT SERVICE CLIENT) is filed under VENTE, RELATIONS CLIENTS and other departments. Check the contract type (CDD or CDI) and the hours in each title. Many of the October 2026 postings are year-end fixed-term roles. For a permanent job, look for CDI titles and the Manager Commerce and Directeur de Magasin postings.

If you're selling or recruiting: Hiring is concentrated in store sales and customer service for the year-end period, with 216 roles opened in the last 30 days against 16 in the prior 30, so seasonal staffing outreach is time-sensitive. Other angles are field technician and delivery-installer hiring, and the seven Devenir Franchisé postings. Requirements were not provided, so confirm them on each posting.
