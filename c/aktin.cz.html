What they're building

Food claims and regulatory compliance strong signal

Today: Site sells supplements and functional foods with nutrition-led claims, e.g. Vitaminové želé bez konzervantů a umělých sladidel and 100 % syrovátky z Nového Zélandu

Roles: Regulatory & Claims Specialist · Product Manager - Pantry Food

Requirements mention: Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 · nutrition and health claims · marketing compliance · Czech food, advertising and consumer protection law · critically evaluate scientific literature

Inference: the company is building a formal claims review step, including an internal library of approved claims, before marketing copy and new food products go live. This rests on two roles that name the EU claims regulations: one in Praha, one in Brno.

Home and self-care assortment moderate signal

Today: Site navigation lists Kosmetika and Domácnost

Roles: Product Manager Self-Care & Cleaning (2) · Product Manager - Home assortment

Requirements mention: product ownership · supplier sourcing · sell-through · launch execution · EU 1223/2009

Inference: the company is growing its existing non-food range with dedicated product managers in Brno. The Self-Care posting covers natural cleaning and personal care; the Home posting covers kitchen, household and sport. Both ask for supplier sourcing and sell-through experience.
