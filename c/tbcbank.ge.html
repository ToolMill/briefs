    How to use this

    If you're applying: About half the open roles (72 of 141) are in Retail, spread across service centres in many towns. There is also a large block of business-lending roles, a smaller set of credit roles for self-employed clients and micro credit groups, and Valuto cashier-operator roles, some on day/night shifts. All roles are marked non-remote. Some postings are reserve pools (რეზერვი/სარეზერვო) and may not be immediate seats. Requirements were not provided, so confirm them in each posting.

    If you're selling or recruiting: 105 roles opened in the last 30 days, against 21 in the prior 30, and none closed in the last 30 days. Most of the volume is in Retail (72 of 141 open roles) and branch-level SME lending. Smaller specialist pockets are: corporate non-credit products (4 roles: deposit product sales, treasury products, a senior cash-management product manager and an administrative assistant); payments/POS terminals (2); TBC Capital and asset management (4); wealth management (2, both in Batumi); technology infrastructure (2); and AI and data (1 data analyst).
