What they're building

Compute: a microVM runtime for agentic workloads, plus multi-region compute capacity strong signal

Requirements mention: Firecracker microVMs · Supabase Compute platform · multi-region infrastructure design · reservation economics

Inference: the Functions (Compute) posting describes Compute as a newly launched microVM architecture for agentic workloads that runs alongside Edge Functions. The two platform roles cover multi-region compute supply in general, not microVMs specifically.

Identity provisioning and machine-to-machine credentials in Supabase Auth moderate signal

Machine-to-machine authentication also appears in the partner integrations PM role. Together these point to directory provisioning and service-to-service credentials as likely next work.
