Seniority: Senior-level roles are most common: 29 senior, 16 director, 15 manager, 9 mid, 5 staff, 3 VP, 4 junior and 1 principal. Most openings are at senior level and above.

These are mostly Account Executive, SDR, sales manager and account manager roles, plus a Workplace Experience Coordinator in Denver.
