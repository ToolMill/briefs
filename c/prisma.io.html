How to use this

If you're applying: These are care and support jobs at the Dutch care organisation Prisma, mostly in Noord-Brabant. They are not software roles at Prisma Data. Most postings ask for an MBO diploma in Zorg en Welzijn (niveau 2 to 5 depending on the role) and irregular hours, often including slaapdiensten and weekends. Pay follows the CAO Gehandicaptenzorg, so check each posting's location, level and shift pattern before applying.

If you're selling or recruiting: Do not use these postings for outreach to Prisma Data, Inc; the record should be separated from the software company. If you are targeting the Dutch care organisation instead, its finance and administration postings in Waalwijk name AFAS, Spend Cloud, Exact, BI tooling, ONS van Nedap and VECOZO. One of those postings describes digitising the administration of about 100 properties together with ICT and Inkoop.
